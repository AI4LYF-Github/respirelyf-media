# Line bank: the voice of every Respire LYF video

The voiceover is the backbone of every video. The picture follows the words, and a weak line can't be saved by a
strong shot. This bank is a **starting point and a quality bar, not a menu**. Don't copy a line in because it's here:
write the line this video needs, and make it at least this good.

## How to write a line (before every voiceover, heading or subtitle)

Think it through before you write. Take your time on every line; this is the work, not a step before it.

1. **Who's watching, and what do they believe at the end?** One sentence each. Most viewers think every app is a
   tracker; every video has to move them past that.
2. **Find the contrast.** What does everyone else do (a tracker, an alert, a video, a meter, a diary)? What does
   Respire LYF do that they don't? The house shape is two beats:
   - "They add devices to the patient. Respire LYF adds intelligence to the devices people already have."
   - "A video teaches the steps; the slip happens at the puff."
3. **Make it concrete.** One image from her real day ("by lunch", "the evening after", "at the puff") beats three
   adjectives. Cut generic lines: "see your patterns", "take control", "powered by AI", "your health, simplified".
4. **Write many, keep one.** Draft at least five versions of each important line. Read them aloud. Keep the one with
   rhythm and a payoff. The food clip took several rounds:
   - raw idea
   - five options
   - a framing chosen
   - a hybrid of two options
   - an expressive take
   That's the normal level of effort, not the exception.
5. **Close the loop.** Check the script reaches Act and points at long-term value: see `video-checklist.md`.
6. **Run the gate.** Check every line with the `respire-lyf-copy` skill:
   - association words only
   - no numbers about outcomes
   - no timed promises
   - the watch named wherever sensing is automatic
   - the pMDI line wherever technique feedback is shown
   A great line that breaks a rule gets rewritten three ways before it's dropped: the feeling almost always survives.
7. **Write for the ear, then the eye.** A voiceover line is spoken: short, natural, one breath. A heading is two beats,
   with line one in ink and line two in accent blue. A subtitle follows the spoken phrase, never a paraphrase.

When you add a line that proves itself in a video, add it here.

## Lines by feature

Each feature has voiceover lines, then a two-beat **heading** (line one / line two).

### Opener and category
- "They add devices to the patient. Respire LYF adds intelligence to the devices people already have."
- "Trackers count. Respire LYF connects." Heading: **Trackers count. / We connect.**
- "A diary remembers what you wrote. Respire LYF remembers what it meant."
- "Most apps keep your data. Respire LYF keeps your patterns."
- "Logging is the homework. Learning is the point."

### Food
- "A food app counts calories. Respire LYF asks what your breakfast had to do with your evening."
- "A tracker would forget it by lunch. Her breathing co-pilot won't." *(the food clip, approved)*
- Heading: **One photo. / Every nutrient.**

### Cough
- "Other trackers tell you how many times you coughed. Respire LYF tells you what else was going on." *(Ali's framing,
  12 Nov 2026)*
- "The count is the start. The context is the story."
- Heading: **The count. / And the context.**

### Inhaler
Name the watch, and add the pMDI line and "Always follow your doctor's guidance", in the same video.
- "A video teaches the steps; the slip happens at the puff."
- "A reminder says it's time. Your watch notices how it went."
- "Technique isn't learned once. It's caught, one puff at a time."
- Heading: **Not just the steps. / The puff.**

### Air and weather
- "An alert tells you what's in the air. Respire LYF learns how your breathing moves with it."
  (not "what that air does to you", which claims a cause)
- "Everyone gets the same pollen count. Only you get your pattern."
- "The forecast is for everyone. The pattern is yours."
- Heading: **Same air. / Your pattern.**

### Sleep
- "Sleep apps score the night. Respire LYF lines it up with the morning after."
- Heading: **Your night. / Your morning.**

### Stress
- "A stress score tells you how today felt. Respire LYF shows what tends to come with it."

### Hydration
- "A water app counts glasses. Respire LYF notices the days you missed them."
- Heading: **Small habit. / Long memory.**

### Peak flow
- "A meter gives you a number. Respire LYF gives it context: the sleep, the air, the week around it."
- "One reading is a snapshot. A month of them is a story."
- Heading: **One reading. / A whole month.**

### Act (the step most videos skip)
- "It doesn't hand you a chart. It hands you one thing to try."
- "Advice is written for everyone. A next step from your own week is written for you."
- "Spotting the pattern is half of it. Trying something is the other half."

### Long-term value
- "Health isn't a moment. It's a pattern."
- "A minute a day. A clearer picture every month."
- "Day one, it starts learning. Every week after, it gets sharper."

### Fingerprint
- "Asthma has a textbook. Your breathing has a fingerprint."
- "Your breathing has a fingerprint. We help you find it." *(the end-card line)*

### Report
- "Your appointment is short. Your month isn't. Bring it."
- "Walk in with the month, not a memory."

### Closing the loop, for any single-feature video
- "Not another tracker. A co-pilot that senses, prompts and learns."
  (not "Not another action plan": a written action plan is the doctor's tool, and the app is adding one)
- Outro: **Download Respire LYF.** → Patent-protected technology. → www.respirelyf.com → partnerships@ai4lyf.com
