## What the user saw (their notes, with timestamps)

1. **0:42.** Frame the co-pilot as a world first.
2. **0:51.** Show Maya before the cough flow. Now it's cough → loop zoom → cough again.
3. **3:40.** Every signal has been explained: the moment to re-engage. The point isn't collecting signals. Every
   breathing has a pattern, a fingerprint invisible to the naked eye, and the goal is finding the hidden combinations.
   Tell that over the donut, with room for storytelling and transitions.
4. **Quiet days.** Make this the wow: a tracker would leave her behind, a co-pilot won't. Imagine still getting
   personalized nudges after three days without logging, from what the platform senses automatically.
5. **4:03.** The MEEP opens before the loop moves to Understand. The loop should step first.
6. **MEEP voice.** Use lined-up language: high pollen, short sleep, lined up with more coughing.
7. **4:16.** The wrong part comes back (the 10 × 5 web) while the MEEP is being explained.
8. **Act.** Base it on how Insights ends, and show LYF following up on what she's testing, in voice and picture.
9. **Report.** Could be better.
10. **Fingerprint.** Frame her individual, repeatable patterns as forming her fingerprint over time. The motion should
    be jaw-dropping. The orbits are fine in the app, but the film can do far more, as the partner film and the website
    do.
11. **Finale.** "This is the kind of loop respiratory care has waited decades for."
12. **End.** "You live your fullest while Respire LYF does the rest. Learn more at respirelyf.com."

Earlier the same day, on look and structure:

## What the review agent found (independent pass)

**Core finding:** the film tells two findings, and the loop's stages alternate between them, so Act and See progress
pay off the wrong one.
- **November, pollen:** the MEEP finds it; Tonight is its action.
- **October, late dinner:** Insights finds it; "dinner before 7:30" is its action. The Report's "What's working" is
  dinner.

Other findings:
- **Insights' card shows early.** Its own "worth trying" card appears before the Act marker.
- **Trends contradicts the MEEP.** "Last week, 2–8 Nov: quieter" against the MEEP's bad nights that same week.
- **The Report comes before the action.** It covers October.
- **The Day Ring spends the reveal.** "Three nights" says it before the MEEP's "4 nights".
- **The fingerprint drops both findings.** Food and weather sit on its weakest ring.
- **Maya's face first appears about 175 s after "This is Maya".**
- **The loop beats step back.** After the Act and See progress markers, the camera returns to the previous page.
- **The MEEP is too fast.** 5.8 s is too short to read.
- **"Those same six nights" are never marked** on the Report.
- **Tracker vs co-pilot is said three times in 20 s.**
- **Several lines read the screen.**
- **Dates disagree.** Phone screens say April. The cough night lands on different nights in different scenes.
  Wednesday is "Excellent" in one place and "Good" in another.
- **Pollen season.** Tree pollen in November.
- **Pacing.** The finale has 14 s with no voice. "Short nights" plays over the Activity stop. Some lines spill into
  the next item.
- **"Sense" is wrong.** The app and website call the first stage **Capture**.

## Decisions

- **One finding carries the loop: late dinner.** Insights finds it (Understand), she tries "dinner before 7:30" and
  LYF checks back (Act), Trends and the Report after the test show what changed (See progress), and it becomes a
  strong thread of her fingerprint (Improve).
- **Saturday's pollen pattern becomes the quiet-days wow:** a nudge that arrives without her logging anything.
- **New order:**
  1. Opening
  2. Maya
  3. Capture
  4. Every signal
  5. The 10 × 5 turn, where the fingerprint is planted
  6. Quiet days and the nudge
  7. Understand (the MEEP, then Insights)
  8. Act, with the follow-up
  9. See progress
  10. Improve (the fingerprint rebuilt in yarn)
  11. The loop seen whole
  12. End card
- **Process:** lines first, through the line bank's "Iterate every line": five drafting agents on five angles, then a
  separate critic, then the user picks. The picture is rebuilt to the locked lines.
- **Rename "Sense" to "Capture".**

## After (13 Nov 2026, picture rebuilt; new lines not yet voiced)

**Lines.** Five drafting agents wrote three drafts per beat, one angle each: contrast, a moment in her day, patience,
the viewer's thought, one surprising image. A separate critic scored all 75 and proposed one set that reads as a single
script. The user chose that set. The record is in the film repo's `storyboard/lines/` (`brief.md`, `draft-*.md`,
`critic.md`, `locked.md`). Lessons from the critic, now rules in `../story-audit.md`:
- **A device budget across the whole film:** questions, "not X, Y" and "thread" each used once or twice at most.
- **One spine of words:** "you stop" → "Trackers stop when you do" → "She stopped" → "a clue worth testing" → "The test
  happens in her kitchen" → "a clue, tested… becomes a thread" → "Hers."
