# Story shape: the starting point for every video

Start every video from this shape, whether it's a 30-second single-signal clip or the full demo. Fill it in, then make
it better. It exists because of the 12 Nov 2026 meeting:
- **A single-signal video** (peak flow, cough, food) must always end by showing that we don't just track that signal:
  we connect it with the whole bigger picture. This outro goes in every video, because it's what makes the product clear.
- **The loop must be complete,** whether the video is about one feature or food. Without the five steps everything
  is confusing, because the market is tired of diaries.
- **The contrast:** others tell you to buy this device and that one. We turn the devices you already have into
  intelligence, and the signal in the video is the beginning, not the end.

## The beats

The five-step loop is the spine (Capture → Understand → Act → See progress → Improve). The bigger-picture outro
closes it, then the call to action.

| # | Beat | What it does | 30 s | 60 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | The contrast in two beats: what everyone else does, then what Respire LYF does | 0–3 | 0–5 |
| 2 | **Capture** | How the signal gets in, automatic first (her watch, Apple Health, the air around her) | 3–8 | 5–15 |
| 3 | **Understand** | The signal meets its context: it lines up with last night's sleep, today's air | 8–13 | 15–27 |
| 4 | **Act** | One thing to try, from her own week | 13–16 | 27–34 |
| 5 | **See progress → Improve** | Days add up and turn green; it keeps learning her | 16–20 | 34–42 |
| 6 | **The bigger picture** | Not a single-signal tracker: every signal, day by day, into her Breathing Fingerprint | 20–26 | 42–53 |
| 7 | **Call to action** | Download Respire LYF → logo, www.respirelyf.com, "Patent-protected technology." → L8 → partnerships@ai4lyf.com | 26–30 | 53–60 |

Beats 4 and 5 can be short, but they are never skipped. A video that stops at "it noticed" isn't finished.

## The bigger-picture outro (beat 6)

Every video gets this beat, and a single-signal video needs it most. It moves through three steps, as one camera move:

1. **Zoom out: one signal among all of them.** The signal's icon, the hand-off element, pulls back and joins the
   donut of every signal. Hold the donut for at least 2 seconds. The point: breathing is never one signal.
2. **Day by day.** The signals weave into her days, and the days into a month she can see (the day ring, or a calendar
   turning green). The point: it's continuous, not a one-off reading.
3. **The fingerprint.** The links that are only hers become her Breathing Fingerprint. The point: intelligence from
   the devices she already has, and it keeps learning.

Then the call to action. In motion, the signal's icon is the thread through all three steps, so the outro reads as a
continuation and never as a separate ending (see `motion.md`).

## Worked example: peak flow, 30 seconds

These lines are **starting drafts to iterate**, not lines to copy. Run every one through "Iterate every line" in
`line-bank.md`.

| Beat | Picture | Voice (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | A meter's number, alone on a white page | "A meter gives you a number. Respire LYF gives it the week around it." |
| Capture | She drags the gauge; a connected meter syncs through Apple Health | "Drag the gauge, or let your meter sync." |
| Understand | The reading lines up with last night's sleep and the pollen | "Then it lines the reading up with your night and your air." |
| Act | One card: a thing to try | "And hands you one thing to try." |
| See progress | A month of readings, the days turning green | "Day by day, you watch the month take shape." |
| Bigger picture | The peak flow icon pulls back into the donut, then the day ring, then the fingerprint | "Peak flow is one thread. Respire LYF weaves in every other, day by day, into a fingerprint that's only yours." |
| Call to action | Download Respire LYF → logo, website, badge → L8 → partnerships | (on screen; the voice can say "Download Respire LYF.") |

The same shape works for cough ("the count is one thread"), food, inhaler, sleep and the rest. Only beats 1–5 change.

## Before you start

- Agree the beats and their seconds with the user before writing lines or code.
- Write the lines with `line-bank.md`, iterated, and check them with the `respire-lyf-copy` skill.
- Build each beat as a named segment of a cut (`GUIDE.md`, "Versions are cuts"), so a 30-second and a 60-second
  version can come from the same scenes.
- Tick `video-checklist.md` before calling it finished.
