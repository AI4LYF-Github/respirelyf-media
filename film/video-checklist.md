# Every Respire LYF video: the must-haves

From the team meeting of 12 November 2026 ("Respire Life retention, onboarding and video strategy"), as Ali set them,
plus the rules this film already keeps. A video isn't finished until every box below is ticked. Words are the backbone:
read `line-bank.md` (and how to write a line, at its top) before writing any voiceover, heading or subtitle.

## Story

- [ ] **The whole loop, every time.** Capture → Understand → Act → See progress → Improve (the app's own five stages,
      onboarding V8). Even a single-feature video (food, cough, peak flow) shows the loop. A video that stops at "it
      noticed" or at tracking isn't finished. Without the loop, everything is confusing for the user.
- [ ] **Started from `story-shape.md`:** hook, the five-step loop, the bigger-picture outro, then the call to action.
- [ ] **Bigger picture for one signal.** A video about one signal ends by tying it to the rest: "Other trackers tell you
      the cough count. Respire LYF puts it in the bigger picture." Use the outro in `story-shape.md`:
      1. the signal joins the donut of every signal, held for at least 2 seconds
      2. day by day
      3. her Breathing Fingerprint
- [ ] **End on long-term value.** Health is long-term and continuous; the last beat says so. The model is the 31-second
      food clip:
      - The first 10 seconds are the wow.
      - Then the patience beat: it keeps connecting, over time.
      - That way one clip drives both sign-ups and retention.
- [ ] **Show what the app gathers itself:** location, weather, pollen, air. Never "add notes" as the way context gets in;
      notes are optional because most of the context is collected anyway.
- [ ] **Better than a tracker, made visible.** Some 30-second videos playfully contrast us with plain trackers,
      comparison-ad style.
- [ ] **Shown, not written.** Whatever can be shown visually isn't shown as text.

## Words

- [ ] Every line goes through the `respire-lyf-copy` skill, and through "How to write a line" in `line-bank.md`.
- [ ] **Every line iterated** ("Iterate every line" in `line-bank.md`):
      - several framings, drafted in parallel by agents
      - a separate critique
      - the user picks from the best options
      - a hybrid, then a performance pass

      No first drafts in a finished video.
- [ ] **Association words only:** "lines up with", "moves with", "tends to come with". Never triggers, causes, predicts,
      or "does to you".
- [ ] **No numbers** about users, outcomes or accuracy. No timed promises ("in a week", "by month one").
- [ ] **Watch:** on screen and in the voice, a **"compatible watch"** or "her watch", never "Apple Watch" (Ali,
      12 Nov 2026; older videos get fixed too). Automatic or passive sensing still names the watch in the same video,
      because it's the watch that makes it automatic.
- [ ] **Inhaler technique feedback** carries the pMDI line in the same video (pMDIs used directly, without a spacer) and
      "Always follow your doctor's guidance." Never show a spacer with automatic technique feedback.

## Outro (every video)

- [ ] **Download Respire LYF** (the call to action, first).
- [ ] Logo, the website **www.respirelyf.com** (everywhere), and **Patent-protected technology.**
- [ ] One disclaimer about healthy lifestyle choices. Use the locked line L8: "Respire LYF promotes and tracks healthy
      lifestyle choices that, as part of a healthy lifestyle, may help people live well with asthma or COPD."
- [ ] **partnerships@ai4lyf.com** at the very end.

## Badge

- [ ] **Patent-protected technology.** sits on every inhaler scene, every cough scene and the outro (Ali: the patent
      line goes everywhere). The wording is exact: never "patented", "patent-pending" or a patent number.

## Motion

- [ ] **Every transition designed:** a camera move that says how two things connect, with one element handed across.
      Never a cut, fade or wipe. See `motion.md`.
- [ ] **Motion everywhere:** no frozen frame. Held shots breathe, values count, charts draw, chips stagger in, exits
      recede.
- [ ] **No app screenshots or recordings, ever.** Every screen is rebuilt in code, in pieces, and animated.
- [ ] **Checked in motion** in Studio, every transition scrubbed frame by frame, and the whole film watched once muted.

## Look and format

- [ ] Light mode, SF Pro 800, big type, one take: the must-haves in `GUIDE.md`.
- [ ] **1080p at 60 fps:** 1920 × 1080 for 16:9, 1080 × 1920 for 9:16. Never 30 fps.
- [ ] **Cuts from the start:** every version and every idea is a named cut under `Cuts`, with every segment named.
- [ ] **Vertical where possible** for social and in-app, but only if the value proposition still comes through. It's a
      `-Vertical` composition of the same cut, laid out again for 9:16, never a crop.
- [ ] **Wow moments look real** (the food rating, the nutrients). The rest can be CGI.
- [ ] **COPD videos** use middle-aged and older characters people can relate to.

## The demo film specifically

- [ ] **One subject throughout.** Photoreal is the tentative pick, with one other style kept as backup.
- [ ] **Three-day montage:** three days she logs nothing, with co-pilot nudges from auto-tracking.
- [ ] **Colour grade:** dull when she feels unwell, brighter and bluer when she's better.
- [ ] **Scenes:** include night cough and inhaler use.
- [ ] **Voice:** ElevenLabs latest model, expressive, as voice-over (not on camera).
- [ ] **Ending:** "Live Your Fullest" → LYF → "Respire LYF".
